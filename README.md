## 同一手机号创建新账户/多账户切换
### 方法1：删除旧账户后重建
1. 访问Telegram停用页面：https://my.telegram.org/auth
2. 输入手机号验证后，注销原有账户（数据会清空）
3. 重新打开Telegram，使用同一手机号注册新账户

### 方法2：多账户切换（需不同手机号）
1. 打开Telegram → 左上角菜单 → 点击当前手机号
2. 选择「添加账户」，输入新手机号完成注册
3. 点击手机号即可自由切换多个账户

## New Account with Same Number / Multi-Account Switch
### Method 1: Delete Old Account and Recreate
1. Visit Telegram deactivation page: https://my.telegram.org/auth
2. Enter phone number to verify, then delete existing account (all data will be cleared)
3. Reopen Telegram, register new account with the same phone number

### Method 2: Multi-Account Switch (Requires Different Phone Numbers)
1. Open Telegram → Menu (top left) → Tap current phone number
2. Select "Add Account", enter new phone number to complete registration
3. Tap phone number to freely switch between multiple accounts

## 常见问题FAQ
### Q1：可以用邮箱注册Telegram吗？
A：不可以，Telegram仅支持手机号作为核心验证方式，邮箱仅用于辅助账户找回。

### Q2：一个手机号可以创建多个Telegram账户吗？
A：不可以，Telegram限制每个手机号仅绑定一个账户；多账户需使用不同手机号。

### Q3：虚拟手机号注册安全吗？
A：选择正规付费VoIP服务商（如Google Voice）相对安全，免费虚拟号存在信息泄露风险。

### Q4：Telegram可以更换绑定手机号吗？
A：可以，在设置中可更换绑定手机号，不会丢失聊天记录和联系人。

## Frequently Asked Questions
### Q1: Can I register Telegram with email?
A: No. Telegram only supports phone number as core verification method. Email is only for account recovery assistance.

### Q2: Can I create multiple Telegram accounts with one phone number?
A: No. Telegram limits one account per phone number. Multiple accounts require different phone numbers.

### Q3: Is it safe to register with virtual phone number?
A: It's relatively safe with regular paid VoIP providers (like Google Voice). Free virtual numbers have information leakage risks.

### Q4: Can I change my bound phone number in Telegram?
A: Yes. You can change bound phone number in Settings without losing chat history or contacts.
